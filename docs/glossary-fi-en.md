Editorial term pairs for Research Evidence Explorer. Canonical workspace text stays Finnish; English is a presentation overlay. Do not strengthen claims, imply validation, or invent a composite trust score when translating.

## Constructed analytical example

A **constructed analytical example** (*rakennettu analyyttinen esimerkki*) organises task–evidence inspection for illustration. It is not an empirical study result, not a validated causal model, and not a clinical safety score. Exports and on-screen tags must keep this designation visible in both languages.

## Expert dimensions vs audited coverage

Keep these families separate in both languages:

- **Expert dimensions** (five separate ratings): perceived trustworthiness, usefulness, source transparency, expert-rated source sufficiency, and perceived verifiability. They are provisional rubrics until validation evidence supports stronger language. They must not be collapsed into a composite trust score.
- **Audited coverage / support layers**: collection coverage, supplied-context sufficiency, answer coverage, claim-support labels, and displayed citation/locator checks. These are recorded audits of evidence structure, not interchangeable stages with expert ratings and not guaranteed outcomes.

**Expert-rated source sufficiency** (an assessor’s judgement that sources feel sufficient for the task) is not the same as **audited evidence coverage** (facet-level marks that required information is present in collection, context or answer). Translation must preserve that distinction.

## Other qualifications to preserve

- Proposed criteria and untested propositions remain provisional / hypothesis-status wording.
- Unmeasured ratings stay explicitly unmeasured (`not-measured`).
- Inference limits and boundaries travel with the record; empty cells mean “not recorded”, not a proven research gap.
